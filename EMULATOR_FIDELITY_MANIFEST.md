## Operational Guidelines for Developers

1.  **Zero-Cost Testing**: Use the standard `connect-rpc` clients. The fleet's `go.work` and `go.mod` automatically route requests to these local handlers when the `EMULATOR_MODE=true` environment variable is set.
2.  **Sovereign Persistence**: All data is stored locally in `@SCRATCH` or the workspace's BoltDB files (`storage.db`, `vault.db`).
3.  **Mandate Adherence**: All emulators use structured logging and comply with Section 0.0 Hierarchical Isolation mandates.
